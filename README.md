# PART I — PROJECT FOUNDATION

## 01. PROJECT OVERVIEW | TỔNG QUAN DỰ ÁN

**VI — Mục tiêu**

Thiết kế lại website DIGO EduTech thành một trải nghiệm thương hiệu doanh nghiệp có khả năng nhận diện cao, kể câu chuyện rõ ràng và dẫn dắt người dùng đến đúng giải pháp.

Website không chỉ là trang giới thiệu dịch vụ. Đây phải là trung tâm kết nối ba năng lực của DIGO:

- DIGO Academy — Đào tạo thực chiến.
- DIGO Growth — Quản trị Performance Ads đa kênh.
- DIGO Infra & Trust — Hạ tầng CAPI, AI Agent và xác minh tài sản số.

Website phải thể hiện DIGO như một hệ sinh thái thống nhất, nơi tri thức, triển khai tăng trưởng và hạ tầng dữ liệu được kết nối với nhau.

**EN — Objective**

Redesign DIGO EduTech into a distinctive corporate digital experience that communicates the brand clearly, demonstrates its capabilities, and guides users toward relevant solutions.

The website must connect three complementary business pillars:

- DIGO Academy — Practical education.
- DIGO Growth — Multichannel performance marketing.
- DIGO Infra & Trust — CAPI infrastructure, AI agents, and digital asset verification.

The result should feel like one coherent ecosystem, not three unrelated businesses.

## 02. REFERENCE STRATEGY | CHIẾN LƯỢC THAM CHIẾU

Use The Coca-Cola Company website as a strategic reference for:

1. A consistent and recognizable brand identity.
2. Strong corporate storytelling.
3. Clear information architecture.
4. Editorial content that builds long-term brand value.
5. Consistent visual rules across digital touchpoints.
6. A scalable content and design system.

**Do not copy:** Coca-Cola's logo, Spencerian lettering, Dynamic Ribbon, Arden Square, distinctive proprietary compositions, or exact page layouts.

**Translate into DIGO:** a proprietary visual system inspired by measurement, data signals, feedback loops, technical precision, and evidence-led growth.

**Creative direction:**  
**Iconic Brand System × Editorial Storytelling × Growth Engineering**

| Color | Hex | Primary role |
|---|---|---|
| Alabaster Clay | #F7F5F0 | Main light background |
| Obsidian Basalt | #0F0E0E | Main text and dark surfaces |

- 60% Alabaster Clay.
- 30% Obsidian Basalt.
- 7% Dynasty Crimson.
- 3% Sovereign Gold.

Treat these proportions as the brand guideline's starting rule, not a requirement to match every page mathematically.

### UI color tokens
Create Figma variables for:
- Brand colors.
- Text primary and secondary.
- Surface primary and secondary.
- Borders and dividers.
- Interactive states.
- Error, warning, and success states.

Semantic status colors may be introduced only as functional UI colors, not as replacements for the brand palette. Validate contrast and accessibility.

Any additional brand color must receive explicit brand approval.

## 19. TYPOGRAPHY | HỆ THỐNG CHỮ

Preserve the established typefaces:
- Plus Jakarta Sans — headings and display typography.
- Be Vietnam Pro — body text and Vietnamese editorial content.
- JetBrains Mono — numbers, technical labels, and data.

- Display: 64–80 px desktop; 40–48 px mobile.
- H1: 48–64 px desktop; 36–40 px mobile.
- H2: 36–48 px desktop; 28–32 px mobile.
- H3: 24–32 px desktop; 22–26 px mobile.

- Use tabular numerals for aligned metrics.

- Define line-height and text spacing tokens.
- Keep editorial hierarchy consistent across VI and EN versions.

## 22. LAYOUT & SPACING | BỐ CỤC

Use an editorial grid with modular components rather than a rigid card-only system.

### Desktop
- 12-column grid.
- Maximum content width proposed at 1,280–1,440 px.
- Consistent horizontal margins.
- Generous vertical spacing between narrative sections.

### Tablet
- 8-column grid.
- Simplified multi-column layouts.
- Clear transition from editorial composition to stacked content.

### Mobile
- 4-column grid.
- Single-column reading flow.
- Large, legible headlines.
- Touch-friendly controls.
- No horizontal overflow.
- Simplified data visualizations.

### Bento usage
Retain Bento modules where they clarify grouped services, features, or evidence. Do not use Bento as the default structure for every section.

## 23. MOTION & INTERACTION | CHUYỂN ĐỘNG VÀ TƯƠNG TÁC

### Allowed motion
- Subtle reveal transitions.
- Short hover elevation.
- Signal-path animations.
- Carefully controlled diagram transitions.
- Gentle image transitions.

### Motion principles
- Motion must communicate hierarchy, cause-and-effect, or navigation.
- Avoid animation that delays access to content.
- Support reduced-motion preferences.
- No flashing or distracting continuous loops.
- No autoplay audio.
- Maintain stable layout while assets load.

### Interaction states
Define default, hover, focus, active, disabled, loading, success, and error states where relevant.

# PART VI — BILINGUAL CONTENT SYSTEM

## 26. VI/EN CONTENT GOVERNANCE | QUẢN TRỊ NỘI DUNG SONG NGỮ

### Language behavior
- Every core page must have a Vietnamese and an English version.
- The language switcher must preserve the equivalent page where a translation exists.
- If a translation is unavailable, route users clearly rather than displaying a broken page.
- Do not automatically mix Vietnamese and English within the same paragraph unless the term is intentionally technical.

### Content parity
Each language version must preserve:
- Meaning.
- Offer scope.
- Metric definitions.
- Limitations.
- CTA intent.
- Legal meaning.

Do not allow translation to strengthen a claim or introduce a guarantee absent from the source.

### CMS fields
For each content item, include:
- Content ID.
- Vietnamese title.
- English title.
- Vietnamese body.
- English body.
- Category.
- Author.
- Source references.
- Publication status.
- Review status.
- Last updated date.
- SEO title and description per language.

# PART VII — EVIDENCE & CLAIM GOVERNANCE

## 27. CLAIMS REGISTER | SỔ ĐĂNG KÝ TUYÊN BỐ

Create a claims register before publishing any performance number, client result, technical guarantee, testimonial, or award.

### Required fields
- Claim ID.
- Exact claim text.
- Language.
- Claim category.
- Evidence source.
- Source owner.
- Measurement period.
- Metric definition.
- Approval owner.
- Publication permission.
- Review date.
- Status: Draft / Under Review / Approved / Expired / Rejected.

### Claims that require verification
Any historical figures in the existing brand guideline, including audited advertising budgets, CPL/CPA values, revenue figures, landing-page speed, gross margin, or other performance metrics, must be reviewed before external publication.

The existence of a figure in a design source does not by itself establish that it is current, independently verified, or approved for public use.

### Publication rule
- Approved claim: may be published within its approved context.
- Unverified claim: do not publish as fact.
- Illustrative number: label clearly as an example.
- Expired claim: remove or revalidate.
- Confidential claim: do not publish without authorization.

This rule applies to both Vietnamese and English versions.

# PART VIII — TECHNICAL UX & ACCESSIBILITY

## 28. RESPONSIVE DESIGN | THIẾT KẾ ĐA THIẾT BỊ

Design and test at minimum:
- Desktop: 1440 px.
- Laptop: 1280 px.
- Tablet: 768 px.
- Mobile: 390 px.
- Narrow mobile: 320–360 px where practical.

These are proposed Figma review frames, not a substitute for testing real responsive behavior.

### Requirements
- No clipped content.
- No unintended horizontal scrolling.
- Navigation works with keyboard and touch.
- Text remains readable at smaller widths.
- Tables and diagrams have mobile-friendly alternatives.
- CTA hierarchy remains clear.
- Forms remain usable without zooming or precision gestures.

## 29. ACCESSIBILITY | KHẢ NĂNG TIẾP CẬN

Target WCAG 2.2 AA where applicable.

Design requirements:
- Sufficient color contrast.
- Visible keyboard focus.
- Semantic heading hierarchy.
- Descriptive link and button labels.
- Form labels and useful error messages.
- Alt-text guidance for meaningful images.
- Captions or transcripts for relevant media.
- Reduced-motion support.
- No reliance on color alone to communicate status.

Accessibility must be checked during design and implementation, not postponed until launch.

## 30. PERFORMANCE & SEO | HIỆU NĂNG VÀ SEO

Design with implementation constraints in mind:
- Responsive and optimized images.
- Controlled font loading.
- Limited decorative animation.
- Clear content hierarchy.
- Search-friendly page titles and descriptions.
- Logical internal linking.
- Canonical and language-alternate handling where appropriate.
- Structured data only where valid and supported by page content.

Any target for page speed must be treated as an acceptance criterion only after measurement methodology, test conditions, and target devices have been defined. Do not display a speed claim merely because it appears in the brand guideline.
